Phase 0 — Discovery & Contracting (Audience: CEO)

We established the legal, commercial and data foundations — Engagement Pack signed, Payment received, DPA countersigned (20 Oct 2025). GA4 & GSC access authorised, enabling immediate baselining.

  • Outcome: Contract & data gating complete.
  • Next gate: Baseline crawl & finalise 50 intents (Phase 1).
  • Evidence: Engagement Pack (signed), Payment confirmation, DPA, Kickoff notes.
Phase 1 — Setup & Baselines (CEO)

Confirmed DPA, GA4/GSC access, baseline crawls and the 50-intent plan. Editorial SOP starters and staging environment prepared.

Client KPIs: Baseline APS; Quality snapshot; 10 pilot pages selected for Wave-1.

Phase 2 — Build & Pilot (Pilot / Day-30 Budget Gate)

We convert diagnostics into an operational patch: ETL (Colab) → Diagnostics_Log (Sheets) → Companion GPT action / Action_Log → staged injection → pilot metrics. Day-30 is the budget gate driven by KPIs below.

  • Key deliverables: Build Dash, Value Easter Egg AI, Companion GPT & API endpoints, Diagnostics_Log, Action_Log, Patch_Package_v1
  • KPIs: APS ≥ 0.60; Quality ≥ 80/100; Non-brand sessions target
  • Evidence required: Diagnostics_Log snapshot, SME sign-offs, staging QA report.
Phase 3 — Stabilise & Adoption

Companion GPT production, enablement workshops, Analytics Patch pipeline documented and operational. Focus on adoption — clinics, office hours and SOP card distribution.

Deliverables: Companion GPT UAT report, 2 enablement workshops, runbook & SLA draft.

Phase 4 — Scale & Operations

Quarterly cadence running, Q→Q comparisons, backlink program scaling and handover. SLA for ongoing patching and governance in place.
